| KPI | Illustrative target |
|---|---:|
| Reach | 250,000 |
| Impressions | 350,000 |
| Engagement rate | 4–6% |
| Website visits | 3,000 |
| Attributed orders | 150 |
| Campaign duration | 4 weeks |

Targets are not achieved results, forecasts or guarantees.

## KPI formulas
- Engagements = likes + comments + shares + saves.
- Engagement rate = engagements / reach × 100.
- CTR = tracked link clicks / impressions × 100.
- Conversion rate = attributed orders / website sessions × 100.
- Cost per engagement = total campaign cost / engagements.
- Cost per order = total campaign cost / attributed orders.
- ROAS = attributed revenue / paid media spend.

## Tracking setup
Use unique UTM links and creator-specific discount codes. Agree on the attribution window and treatment of organic versus paid distribution before launch.
