# AUREL — Brand and Market Brief

## Brand snapshot
AUREL is a fictional, digital-first Indian fashion label offering accessible contemporary clothing for young adults. Its proposed assortment includes versatile tops, relaxed co-ords, casual dresses and styling essentials.

## Positioning
For style-conscious young adults who want versatile looks without overcomplicating their wardrobes, AUREL offers trend-aware everyday fashion for college, casual and social settings.

## Target audience
- Primary age: 18–28.
- Geography: Indian Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities.
- Interests: fashion discovery, styling, beauty, lifestyle and online shopping.
- Purchase considerations: price, fit, styling versatility, reviews, creator recommendations and return policy.
- Discovery channels: Instagram Reels, creator content, short-form video and peer recommendations.

## Marketing challenge
A new fashion label competes with established brands, marketplaces and creator-led labels for attention. The challenge is to build recognition and product consideration without relying only on discounts.

## Consumer insight (hypothesis)
A useful styling demonstration may provide more value than a simple product showcase because viewers can visualise how a piece fits into different occasions. This is a hypothesis to test, not a verified research finding.

## Campaign proposition
**#StyleYourWay:** One product, multiple looks, different occasions. Creators demonstrate how selected pieces can be styled in ways that suit their own personal style.

## Brand voice
Confident, contemporary, approachable and visually expressive. Avoid hard-sell language, exaggerated claims and scripted endorsements.

## Research required before a real launch
- Validate target segments through customer interviews or survey data.
- Review competitor pricing, assortment, content formats and positioning.
- Confirm product availability, size range, delivery and return experience.
- Validate platform preferences and creator categories with customer data.
