# Influencer Evaluation Framework

The model uses five weighted criteria. Rate each candidate from 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent).

| Criterion | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience-brand relevance | 30% | Audience age, geography, interests and brand fit |
| Engagement quality | 25% | Meaningful comments, saves, shares and consistency |
| Content quality | 20% | Clarity, storytelling, styling and creator-native execution |
| Audience authenticity | 15% | Manual review of audience quality, suspicious spikes and comment patterns |
| Cost efficiency | 10% | Deliverables and cost relative to fit and quality |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

## Formula
For each criterion: `(score / 5) × weight`

**Weighted score / 100** = sum of all five weighted criterion scores.

Example: a creator scoring 4/5 for relevance receives `4 ÷ 5 × 30 = 24` points for that criterion.

## Decision rules
- Treat the score as decision support, not automatic approval.
- Manually inspect audience geography and comment quality.
- Review follower growth patterns and past brand collaborations.
- Check brand safety, disclosure practices, availability and rates.
- Keep source URLs and the date each metric was checked.

## Important
The creator names and any sample metrics in this project are fictional placeholders. Do not treat them as real creator recommendations or verified data.
