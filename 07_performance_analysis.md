# Performance Analysis Notes

The accompanying CSV is a **synthetic practice dataset**. It demonstrates calculations and dashboard structure; it is not campaign performance.

## Suggested analysis
1. Compare reach, impressions and engagement by creator tier.
2. Calculate engagement rate using engagements divided by reach.
3. Compare clicks and orders by creator.
4. Calculate cost per engagement and cost per order.
5. Compare attributed revenue with campaign cost and paid media spend.
6. Identify which content format appears strongest in the sample.

## Dashboard suggestions
**Cards:** total reach, impressions, engagements, clicks, orders and spend.  
**Charts:** engagements by creator, CTR by content format, orders by creator tier, spend vs attributed revenue.  
**Filters:** creator tier, content format and campaign week.

## Interpretation caution
A small synthetic dataset cannot establish causality or predict real campaign outcomes. In a live campaign, validate attribution, deduplicate reach where possible, account for organic and paid distribution separately, and compare against a baseline.
