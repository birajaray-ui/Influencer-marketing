# Campaign Creative Briefs

**Campaign:** #StyleYourWay  
**Brand:** AUREL (fictional)  
**Tone:** confident, expressive, contemporary and relatable.  
**Visual direction:** clean styling, natural movement, strong outfit framing, minimal overlays and creator-native editing.

## Concept 1 — One Piece, Three Ways
**Format:** 20–30 second Reel  
**Hook:** "One piece. Three plans. Which look are you?"  
**Sequence:** show the base item; transition into three styled looks; close with a product tag and campaign hashtag.  
**CTA:** "Save the look you would wear."

## Concept 2 — College to After Hours
**Format:** 15–25 second Reel  
**Hook:** "From lectures to last-minute plans."  
**Sequence:** casual daytime styling; quick accessory or shoe change; evening-ready look.  
**CTA:** "Which version is more you?"

## Concept 3 — Creator's Personal Pick
**Format:** Reel or carousel  
**Hook:** "The piece I keep reaching for this week."  
**Sequence:** creator explains fit, styling and practical use in their own voice.  
**CTA:** "Explore the edit."

## Caption draft
"Same piece, different plans. Styled my way with AUREL. Which look would you pick? #StyleYourWay"

## Creator brief checklist
- Show the product clearly and accurately.
- Use personal language; do not read a rigid script.
- Disclose paid or gifted collaboration clearly where applicable.
- Avoid unsupported claims about quality, sustainability or performance.
- Submit draft content for factual and brand-safety review.
- Confirm music, image and usage rights before publication.
