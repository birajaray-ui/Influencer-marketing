# AUREL — Influencer Marketing Case Study

## Executive summary
This case study proposes a four-week creator-led campaign to build awareness and product discovery for AUREL, a fictional digital-first fashion brand. The #StyleYourWay concept uses creator styling demonstrations to show how selected pieces can be worn across different occasions.

## Strategic recommendation
Use a tiered creator mix: three micro-creators, two mid-tier creators and five nano-creators. Select partners using a weighted framework that prioritises audience relevance and engagement quality over follower count alone.

## Creative approach
- One Piece, Three Ways
- College to After Hours
- Creator's Personal Pick
- Community Styling Prompt

The proposed package includes five Reels, supporting story sets, five nano-creator assets and a brand recap.

## Budget
The illustrative planning budget is **₹1,50,000**, including creator fees, gifting and shipping, paid amplification and contingency.

## Measurement
Proposed planning targets: 250,000 reach, 350,000 impressions, 3,000 website visits and 150 attributed orders. Track creator-level results using UTM links and unique discount codes. These are assumptions, not achieved results or guarantees.

## Role of AI
AI supports research structuring, creator comparison templates, creative ideation and reporting drafts. Human review is essential for creator verification, audience quality, brand safety, legal compliance and final decisions.

## Limitations
AUREL is fictional. Creator profiles, rates, budget allocations, targets and CSV performance data are illustrative. No campaign was executed and no real results are claimed.
