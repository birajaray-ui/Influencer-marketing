# AI-Driven Influencer Marketing Campaign
## AUREL | #StyleYourWay

**Project type:** Independent portfolio case study  
**Industry:** Fashion & lifestyle (hypothetical D2C brand)  
**Market:** India | **Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Status:** Simulated campaign; no live campaign has been executed.

## Overview
This project develops an end-to-end influencer marketing campaign for AUREL, a fictional Indian digital-first fashion label. It demonstrates AI-assisted research, creator evaluation, campaign planning, budgeting and performance measurement.

All creator profiles, rates, budgets, targets and performance data are synthetic or illustrative. No actual creators, audience metrics, campaign results or sales are represented.

## Business challenge
AUREL aims to build awareness and product discovery among fashion-conscious Indian consumers aged 18–28 in a competitive social-commerce environment.

## Campaign concept
**#StyleYourWay** — creators demonstrate multiple ways to style selected pieces for college, casual outings and evening occasions. The concept prioritises useful styling inspiration over repetitive product endorsements.

## Objectives
1. Build brand awareness among the target audience.
2. Drive qualified traffic to product pages.
3. Encourage saves, shares and meaningful engagement.
4. Compare creator-level performance using tracked links and discount codes.

## Tools and AI workflow
- **ChatGPT / generative AI:** ideation, research-question design, brief drafting and performance-summary structuring.
- **Excel:** creator scoring, budget calculations and KPI tracking.
- **Power BI (extension):** creator and campaign dashboarding.
- **Canva (extension):** visual campaign mock-ups.

AI output requires human review. Creator audience metrics, authenticity, rates and availability must be verified before a real campaign.

## Limitations and ethical use
This is a simulated portfolio project, not a client engagement or executed campaign. Targets are planning assumptions, not forecasts or guarantees. Creator profiles and sample data are fictional. Before a real campaign, verify creators manually, secure written deliverable and usage-rights agreements, comply with applicable ASCI and platform disclosure requirements, and protect customer data.

## Future improvements
Replace synthetic profiles with researched public creator accounts, record source URLs and access dates, validate current metrics manually, and build a Power BI dashboard using verified campaign data after execution.
