# Campaign Plan — #StyleYourWay

## Objectives
1. Build awareness for AUREL among fashion-conscious consumers aged 18–28.
2. Generate qualified product-page visits through creator content.
3. Encourage saves, shares, comments and user-generated styling content.
4. Identify creator formats that deliver efficient engagement and traffic.

## Proposed creator mix
- 3 micro-creators: 10K–50K followers each.
- 2 mid-tier creators: 50K–200K followers each.
- 5 nano-creators: 1K–10K followers each.

These are tiers, not named or verified accounts. Selection should prioritise audience fit and content quality over follower count.

## Content pillars
- **One piece, three looks:** demonstrate styling versatility.
- **College to evening:** transition a look between occasions.
- **Creator's personal pick:** first-person styling perspective.
- **Community styling prompt:** invite followers to share their own interpretation.

## Proposed deliverables
- 5 creator Reels (one per micro/mid-tier creator).
- 5 supporting story sets.
- 5 nano-creator short videos or story sets.
- 1 brand-led recap/carousel.
- Reuse creator content only where written usage rights are agreed.

## Four-week timeline
| Week | Workstream | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research, shortlist, outreach, contracting | Approved creator list and briefs |
| 2 | Product dispatch, concept approvals, production | Approved draft content |
| 3 | Launch and community management | Live posts and tracked links |
| 4 | Reminder content, UGC prompt, reporting | Performance review |

## Measurement
Track reach, impressions, video views, saves, shares, comments, link clicks, sessions, orders, revenue, spend and creator-level cost. Use unique UTM links and discount codes. Define attribution windows consistently before launch.

## Risks and compliance
- Obtain written deliverables, timelines, usage rights and exclusivity terms.
- Require clear advertising disclosures under applicable Indian ASCI guidance and platform rules.
- Avoid unsupported product or performance claims.
- Check creator audience quality and brand safety manually.
- Obtain consent before reposting creator content.
- Protect customer data and use aggregated reporting where possible.
